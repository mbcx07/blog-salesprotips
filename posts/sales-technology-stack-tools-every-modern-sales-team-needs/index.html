Sales Technology Stack: Tools Every Modern Sales Team Needs

2026-06-18 · CRM · Tags: Sales Technology, Sales Tools, Tech Stack, Sales Automation

The modern sales team runs on technology. The right tools can double your team's productivity, improve forecasting accuracy, and accelerate deal velocity.

The Foundation: CRM

Your CRM is the central nervous system of your sales operation.

Top choices:
- Salesforce: Enterprise-grade, highly customizable
- HubSpot CRM: User-friendly, great for mid-market
- Pipedrive: Pipeline-focused, visual interface
- Zoho CRM: Affordable, good for growing teams
- Freshsales: AI-powered, built-in communication

Must-have features:
- Contact and deal management
- Pipeline visualization
- Email integration
- Reporting dashboards
- Mobile access

Prospecting and Lead Generation

LinkedIn Sales Navigator: Advanced search, lead recommendations, CRM integration
ZoomInfo: B2B contact database with direct dials and emails
Lusha: Contact data enrichment
Apollo.io: All-in-one prospecting and engagement
Clearbit: Data enrichment and company intelligence

Sales Engagement Platforms

Outreach: Sequence automation, A/B testing, analytics
SalesLoft: Cadence management, call recording, coaching
Groove: Simple sequences, pipeline management
Mixmax: Email tracking, scheduling, templates
Yesware: Email tracking, templates, analytics

Conversation Intelligence

Gong: AI-powered call recording, analysis, and coaching
Chorus: Conversation analytics and deal intelligence
Jiminny: Live coaching and conversation analytics
Wingman: Real-time guidance and call scoring

Revenue Intelligence

Clari: AI-powered forecasting and revenue analytics
Gainsight: Customer success and revenue optimization
InsightSquared: Sales analytics and reporting
People.ai: Activity data and pipeline intelligence

Building Your Stack

Starter Stack (1-10 reps)

  • HubSpot CRM (free)
  • LinkedIn Sales Navigator
  • Lusha
  • DocuSign

Growth Stack (10-50 reps)

  • Salesforce or HubSpot (paid)
  • Outreach or SalesLoft
  • Gong
  • PandaDoc
  • ZoomInfo

Enterprise Stack (50+ reps)

  • Salesforce Enterprise
  • Outreach + SalesLoft
  • Gong + Clari
  • Highspot
  • ZoomInfo + Clearbit

Conclusion

Invest in tools that solve specific problems for your team. Start with the essentials (CRM + prospecting), add engagement tools as you grow, and layer on intelligence tools when you have enough data.
