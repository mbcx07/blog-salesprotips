Sales Metrics That Matter: KPIs Every Sales Leader Should Track

2026-06-28 · Strategy · Tags: Sales Metrics, KPIs, Sales Analytics, Performance Tracking
Not all sales metrics are created equal. Many teams track vanity metrics that look good on paper but don't drive improvement. Here are the metrics that actually matter for sales leaders. ## Leading vs. Lagging Indicators **Leading indicators** predict future performance: - Number of prospecting activities - Pipeline generation rate - Meeting-to-opportunity conversion - Proposal velocity **Lagging indicators** measure past performance: - Revenue closed - Quota attainment - Win rate - Average deal size ## The 10 Sales Metrics That Matter ### 1. Activity Metrics **Calls made per day:** The foundation of outbound sales. **Emails sent per day:** Volume matters, but quality matters more. **Meetings booked per week:** The bridge between activity and pipeline. **Proposals sent per month:** The final step before closing. **Target:** 50+ calls, 30+ emails, 5+ meetings per week per rep. ### 2. Conversion Rates **Lead-to-Opportunity:** What percentage of leads become qualified opportunities? **Opportunity-to-Proposal:** How many opportunities result in proposals? **Proposal-to-Close:** What's your proposal win rate? **Overall Lead-to-Close:** The ultimate conversion metric. ### 3. Pipeline Metrics **Pipeline coverage ratio:** Total pipeline value ÷ quota. Target: 3x-5x. **Deal velocity:** Average days from creation to close. **Stage-to-stage conversion:** Where are deals getting stuck? **Pipeline age:** How old are your deals? ### 4. Revenue Metrics **Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR):** For subscription businesses. **Annual Contract Value (ACV):** Average annual deal size. **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC):** Total sales cost ÷ new customers. **Customer Lifetime Value (LTV):** Revenue from a customer over their lifetime. ### 5. Efficiency Metrics **CAC Payback Period:** How long to recover acquisition costs? **LTV:CAC Ratio:** Target 3:1 or higher. **Sales Capacity:** Maximum revenue your team can produce. **Cost per meeting:** Total outreach cost ÷ meetings booked. ### 6. Team Performance Metrics **Quota attainment:** Percentage of reps hitting quota. **Ramp time:** Months to first quota attainment. **Attrition rate:** Percentage of reps leaving annually. **Promotion rate:** Internal advancement percentage. ### 7. Customer Metrics **Net Revenue Retention (NRR):** Revenue from existing customers. **Churn rate:** Percentage of customers lost. **Expansion revenue:** Upsells and cross-sells. **Customer satisfaction (CSAT):** Post-purchase satisfaction scores. ## Building Your Sales Dashboard **Weekly Dashboard:** - Activities per rep - Pipeline added - Deals closed - Forecast vs. target **Monthly Dashboard:** - Conversion rates - Pipeline coverage - Win rates by segment - Team performance rankings **Quarterly Dashboard:** - Revenue vs. plan - CAC and LTV trends - Attrition and hiring - Strategic initiatives progress ## Conclusion The right metrics give you visibility into your team's performance and guide your decisions. Start with the fundamentals, build your dashboard, and review metrics consistently.
