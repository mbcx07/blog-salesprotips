Objection Handling: Turn Every No into a Yes

2026-07-23 · Techniques · Tags: Objection Handling, Sales Techniques, Closing, Objections
Objections are not rejections — they're requests for more information. The best salespeople don't fear objections; they welcome them as opportunities to demonstrate value and build trust. ## The Psychology of Objections When a prospect raises an objection, they're actually engaged. Indifference is the real enemy. Objections mean they're considering your solution but have concerns that need addressing. ## The LAER Framework **Listen** — Hear the full objection without interrupting. **Acknowledge** — Validate their concern. "I understand why you'd feel that way." **Explore** — Ask clarifying questions to understand the root cause. **Respond** — Address the specific concern with evidence and value. ## The 5 Most Common Objections and How to Handle Them ### 1. "It's too expensive." **Don't:** Immediately discount. **Do:** Reframe the conversation around value and ROI. **Response:** "I understand price is a concern. Let me show you how our clients typically see a 3x return within the first 6 months." ### 2. "I need to think about it." **Don't:** Leave without a next step. **Do:** Identify what specifically needs thinking about. **Response:** "I appreciate you want to be thorough. What specific aspect are you most uncertain about?" ### 3. "We're happy with our current provider." **Don't:** Badmouth the competitor. **Do:** Highlight your unique differentiators. **Response:** "That's great to hear. Many of our clients felt the same way before they discovered we offer [specific advantage]." ### 4. "Now isn't a good time." **Don't:** Disappear. **Do:** Understand their timeline and create urgency. **Response:** "I understand timing is tight. What would change between now and when it becomes a priority?" ### 5. "Send me some information." **Don't:** Send a generic brochure. **Do:** Schedule a specific follow-up. **Response:** "I'd be happy to. To make sure I send the most relevant information, could we schedule a 10-minute call next week?" ## Advanced Objection Handling Techniques **Feel, Felt, Found:** "I understand how you feel. Other clients have felt the same way. What they found was..." **The Boomerang:** Turn the objection into a reason to buy. "That's exactly why you need our solution." **The Question:** Answer an objection with a question that leads the prospect to their own conclusion. ## Building Your Objection Playbook Create a document with: - Every objection you hear - The LAER response for each - Supporting data and case studies - Role-play scripts for practice ## Conclusion Objection handling is a skill that improves with practice. Record your calls, review your responses, and continuously refine your approach.
